Reflect on the differences between static and interactive visualizations.
Static visualizations are quick and easy to make, but you can't interact with them.
Interactive visiualizations let you zoom, hover, and explore the data, which helps you get a 
better understanding of it.

Write a short paragraph discussing the advantages of using dashboards for real-time data exploration.
Dashboards let you explore data in real-time using dropdown and charts. It also allows you to make
better and quicker decisions. They provide a more dynamic way of viewing and interacting with data
most relevant to them.

Explain how interactive tools like Plotly and Dash can improve data communication in professional settings.
They make it easier to share complex data so you can better understand and explore. In addition,
it allows you to explore and find insight and explain the meaning of the data.
